# Tracery — Animated Network & Energy Effect

**Tracery** is a visual effect for Effects Craft that creates dynamic networks of interconnected points and flowing lines around a subject.

Inspired by digital networks, energy fields, and motion graphics, Tracery turns simple point connections into an animated visual element that can add depth and movement to your compositions.

### Features
- **Dynamic Point Detection** — Generates points that form a connected network around the subject.
- **Connected Line Network** — Links points together to create a web-like structure.
- **Animated Signal Flow** — Adds moving particles that travel along the connected lines.
- **Customizable Line Styles** — Choose between different line styles, including straight and curved connections.
- **Custom Line Colors** — Adjust the network's appearance with your own colors.
- **Adjustable Detection Area** — Control the area in which points and connections are generated.
- **Customizable Line & Point Size** — Fine-tune the thickness of the lines and the size of the points.
- **Coordinate Display** — View the X and Y coordinates of individual points.

### Made for Motion Graphics
Whether you're creating futuristic HUD elements, digital character effects, energy networks, or abstract motion graphics, Tracery gives you a flexible way to build animated interconnected designs directly inside Effects Craft.
